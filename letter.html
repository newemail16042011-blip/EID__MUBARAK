A Letter For You

I know you might be scolding me right now, or thinking that all of this is nonsense… yet my heart insists that I speak, so here I am saying it.

Even today, I still don't fully understand this feeling… I never met you in real life, never saw you face to face. And I know that maybe you never truly loved me either. Still, somehow, after you left, my heart never made space for anyone else. Sometimes I wonder what kind of feeling this is… one that is both hard to understand and impossible to forget.

I had once told you something — just as I love my mother with truth, respect, and all my heart, I love you with the same honesty and sincerity. And no one can ever take my mother's place… perhaps that is why giving your place to someone else feels impossible for me as well.

And one thing I wholeheartedly acknowledge… you are truly an incredible person. Not just good, but extraordinarily good. I don't just say this — I feel it. When you loved me, your love was pure, real, and unmatched. You are someone who loves not just with words, but with your heart.

Your character is like pure gold — honest, clean, and precious. Honestly, I would even call it a diamond: strong, shining, flawless. In today's world, a heart this pure and a character this genuine is extremely rare.

I could call you the moon, but even the moon has its flaws… your character has none. That is why you seem more beautiful to me than the moon itself. True beauty doesn't lie in the face but in the heart and intentions — and your heart is unbelievably pure.

Yes, you are a little crazy sometimes 😄, but it's the kind of playful craziness that makes anyone smile. Maybe that's why you feel so real — completely genuine, utterly authentic.

I still remember when you told me, "Never even think about a second marriage in life." I laughed and said, "I don't know if the first one will even happen." At the time, it felt like a joke… but now I realize that those words carried your care and genuine love.

Honestly, one thing about you has always stayed with me: your heart is pure, your intentions are pure, and your character is invaluable. In a world full of superficiality, true value lies in a person's character — and yours is one of those rare characters that commands respect naturally.

Wherever you are, and with whoever you are, I pray that Allah always keeps you happy. May your life be filled with peace, respect, and every joy you deserve. I pray from the bottom of my heart that Allah protects you from every sorrow and makes every path in your life easy and bright.

And yes… a little crazy thing to add: I am still waiting for you, and I will continue to wait. I know maybe you'll never come back… but who can stop a heart from hoping?

Who knows… at some twist of life, someday, you might come back. Whether it's 10 years later, 20 years later… or even 60 years later.

If I'm still alive then, I will probably just smile and quietly say:

"Look… I'm still right here." 💕
